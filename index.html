1Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Repost, Saved & Like Cleaner for Instagram (“the Extension”) uses and stores information when you use it to manage your reposts, saved posts and likes on Instagram. The Extension works entirely inside your browser. Your Instagram account data is never sent to the developer, sold or shared.

2Information the Extension Accesses

  • Your reposts, saved posts and likes: read on instagram.com so the Extension can show them, with each post’s picture, creator, type and date, and remove a repost, unsave or unlike only the posts you select.
  • Post details: when you open a post, the Extension shows its public details, such as date, likes, comments, views, place, tagged people and caption.
  • Your signed-in session: the Extension uses your existing Instagram login to carry out the actions you request. It never reads, collects or stores your password.
  • License key: if you buy the full version, the key you enter is used only to confirm your purchase.
  • Support messages (optional): if you use “Report a problem”, the Extension sends the message you write, the email address you choose to give, and, if you leave the option on, basic technical details (extension version, browser type and error codes, with no usernames or posts).

All requests go directly to Instagram, in the same way the Instagram website works. The Extension works only on instagram.com and only after you start an action.

3How the Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the Extension’s features:

  • Showing, searching, filtering and sorting your reposts, saved posts and likes.
  • Removing reposts, unsaving saved posts and unliking likes that you choose, while always skipping starred posts.
  • Showing insights, such as posts per month, type, age and top creators.
  • Downloading the photos and videos you choose, and exporting your lists and history.
  • Creating an optional backup page of the posts you remove, before removal starts.
  • Replying to support messages you send.

Everything is processed on your own device, in your browser. No account data is sent to the developer or to any analytics service.

4Data Storage and Retention

The following information is saved only in your browser, on your device:

  • Settings, including speed, view and theme.
  • Your loaded lists of reposts, saved posts and likes.
  • Starred (kept) posts and kept creators.
  • The history of posts you have removed, unsaved or unliked.
  • Your license key and license status.

A free-removal counter is also saved in Chrome’s sync storage, which Chrome may copy to your other browsers signed in to the same Google account, and in the Instagram page’s storage on your device. Photos, videos, backup pages and exports (CSV, JSON, TXT and HTML) are saved only as files on your own device.

Data in the Extension’s storage is kept only while the Extension is installed and is deleted when you uninstall it. The free-removal counter in the Instagram page’s storage remains until you clear your browser’s site data, and files you have saved stay on your device until you delete them.

5Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, except in these two cases, both started by you:

  • Gumroad: when you activate the full version, and about once a week after that, your license key and the product ID are sent to Gumroad, our payment provider, only to confirm that your license is valid.
  • FormSubmit: when you send a “Report a problem” message, it is delivered to the developer’s email through the FormSubmit form service and is used only to reply to you.

No Instagram data is included in either case.

6What We Never Do

  • Sell, rent or transfer user data to third parties.
  • Use data for advertising, profiling or to determine creditworthiness.
  • Use or transfer data for any purpose unrelated to managing your reposts, saved posts and likes on Instagram.
  • Collect analytics, track your browsing or load code from outside the Extension.

7Children’s Privacy

The Extension is not intended for children.
